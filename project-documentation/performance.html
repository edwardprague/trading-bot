Closing Dubious Trades

  • You can take a trade for a valid enough reason, but see that that reason might not be so sound, and close the trade early. There is no harm in that, and can be a very valid and helpful move.
  • You can advantagiously get back into the trade at a more clear time, or reverse your direction.
  • A main area that doesn't get consciously thought about is how clear are impetus factors for the setup?

Psychological Performance Factors

Factor Description
FOMO Situations of uncertianty create fear, in this case specifically FOMO.
Over Thinking Taking too many factors into consideration and muddying the signal with noise, instead of just watching the simple info on the 1m chart 98% of the time and nothing more. I think this goes to backtesting and studying past charts. It's all so spontanious that trying to study more will surely have the adverse and counter-productive effect making me biased, causing over thinking and keeping my eye on the ball. There will be times when you have an idea about a new measurement for improvement. Always consciously mimimize effort there.
Entry Accuracy Sometimes I ‘know’ price will go to a certain level, the best entry level, but I think this is good enough, and I'll still have room, so I don’t have to wait. But this could easilly and rightfully cause me to move SL. I literally get lazy to wait and this is a bad area to get wrong, because it's very important and it doesn't take too much to get right.
Entry Patience and FOMO Near the entry level when poised is a uniquely different mindset. FOMO is most apt to arise so it's important to be extra patient for that best entry as best you can identify. This is especially important after a long string of wins and confidence swells.
Judgement Thinking about if the price will take off without you. Thinking how things should be going. Thinking if I can manage the trade better. Feeing sure or very confident about things. Preparation is nothing, and quality of attention is everything. It's extremely counter-intuitive.
P&L Thinking Don't think about the PL at all during or after trading. Only think about performance, minimally, and just be as vaguely aware as possible about PL.
TOD Expectations It's natural for the brain to expect trades at certian times of day, and to just take a trade based on the time of day. This should be continually nutralized.
Quota Expectations It's vital to consciously not be concerned about a daily or session quota.
No Effort It's vital to apply no effort to trading. To effort to find a trade, catch a trade, or meet a quota goal.
Quality Attention What you're paying attention to, and how open, unbiased, clear, relaxed and patient your attention is, is everything. Preparation is nothing, and quality of attention is everything. It's extremely counter-intuitive. QOA means low attention, unfocused, unambitious, unhurried, and without any mental effort.
Over Trading Trading is an extremely minimal activity, which can create discomfort, and trades would be taken to feel productive. Additionally superfluous trades can naturally be taken for the purposes of testing the strategy and parctice.
Risk Management Giving the trade more room and taking on more risk than initially planned. It's always so tempting.
Revenge Trading Trying to recoup losses ASAP and making rushed choices.
