Induction / Sweep Strategy

Methodology

The price on the 1m chart moves in macro swings which serve 2 purposes: Induction and Sweeping

This is the way that the market makes it's money on the LTF. The PP structure is very clear and consistent on the 1m chart. It's mostly simple to see and it can't foundationally be much more complex.

The sweeps are efficient, quick, and rather consistant.

Swing Types

  • Induction (Slower - More PP) - A large swing with a few handful of PBPP to an HTF PP (At the edge the remaining PP can be left for further buildup).
  • Sweep (Faster - Less PP) - PB swings should sweep a number of previous PBPP with around 3 PP legs.

Trade Types - Both After A Sweep

  • Counter Trend - At the edge of an HTF range, after a strong trend to get there.
  • Trend - An LTF micro trend with a clearish overall direction, and a multi PP leg proper sweeping PB.

Entry Questions

1

Overreaching Questions

- What does the price look like it's doing (to most people) and what is it probably doing (against most people)?

- Did price just do what most people probably thought it would not do?

- How CLEAR are the conditions? Because a trade is like a fart...

2

HTF

What is the HTF PA, and where are the PP? What does the HTF price look like it's doing (to most people) and what is it probably doing?

3

LTF

What direction makes sense on the LTF according to it's PA and the HTF PA?

4

PP Sweeps

Which PP could likely be swept sooner? Which PP could likely be swept later? (Good potential SL level)

5

Trend Entries

If trend tading, how many PBPP has the PB swing had? How many PP sweeps did it do? What is the distance to the further PP, indicating it might not continue there?

6

CT Entries

If CT trading, what HTF PP was swept? Is the price at least TP distance from the swept PP?

Discoveries

  • The JP often does most of it's movement in the Asian session, and is nicely consolidating during London through to NY.
  • When the price pulls back and sweeps the recent PP, there is often a cluster of them rather close with the next furthest PP being a good distance away, which it can tend to leave alone?
  • The NY session often breaks out of the London session HL, so it may be good to be looking for more trend trades in NY and range trades (at top or bottom) during London.
  • CT Trades must be at least TP distance from the swept PP.
  • THE SWEEP OCCURS IN ABOUT HALF THE TIME OF THE INDUCTION?

Considerations

  • You have a mastered methodology about the induction sweep on the 1m. Think on what the 5 and 15m HTF methodology would look like, and hour is probably too much.
  • It seems like the 5m and 15 react in the same way. It seems to be a good factor that on those time frames the PP need more efficient sweeping because those traders aren't in it for long.
  • The question for sweeps is timing, how long does it take to sweep 15 and 5m. There sould be an standard ideal timing element to that.

Trade Examples

🔥 Most Ideal Setups
  • A long induction swing with a few handfuls of PBPP, for about 500+ pips to the bottom of the HTF range.
  • A more rapid PB sweep over about 1 handful of PBPP, leaving 2 or 2 spread out PP, with ideally a larger gap between the further cluster.
  • The PB sweep has 2-3 PP, just what the recipe calls for.
  • Once that further PBPP gets swept, before the larger gap (of about 125 in this example), take the trade.
  • Essentially PP counting. How many PP did the PB seing have? How many PP were swept.
  • In a counter trend trade the entry should be at least TP distance from the sweep.

The entry signal is the sweep with exhaustion.
