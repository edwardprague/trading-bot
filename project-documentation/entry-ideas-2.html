Micro Swing Strategy

Methodology

The price on the 1m chart moves in macro swings which serve 2 purposes: Induction and Sweeping

The PP structure is very clear and consistent.

  • Induction - Swings are composed of at least 2 or 3 PP.
  • Sweep - PB swings should sweep a number of previous PBPP.

Entry Signals

1

Foundational Requirement - Induction / Sweep Swing

After a formidable PB swing has occured, creating a number of PP, and swept a reasonable number of PBPP. The trade can be entered immediately as a counter trend trade.

2

Confluence 1 - Engulfing Candle

An engulfing candle in the expected direction

3

Confluence 2 - Head and Shoulders

A head and shoulders fractal formation. Head and shoulders entry must be preceded by at least 2 PP. This would confirm that it was a long enough PB to appropriately reverse and continue from.

4

Confluence 3 - Change of Charachter

Change of Charachter formation

Trend Filter

  • HTF - 20 SMA
  • LTF - No SMA used which is highly advantagous as they are only conflicting noise on the 1m chart.
