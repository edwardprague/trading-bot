Research & Development

Discovery Development
Bayesian / guided search (e.g. Optuna) instead of pure random
Current Discovery uses pure random sampling — each trial picks parameter combinations independently with no learning from previous trials. Bayesian search (e.g. using the Optuna library in Python) instead builds a probabilistic model of which regions of the search space are promising, and samples more densely there over time. This dramatically improves the pass rate per trial — fewer wasted trials in clearly poor regions, more exploration around combinations that are working. The practical benefit is finding better candidates with the same trial budget, or the same quality candidates with far fewer trials.
Composite objective function scoring
Currently passing is binary — a trial either clears all thresholds (PF ≥ 1.5, min trades, max DD) or it doesn't. All passing trials are shown equally with no ranking beyond PF sort. A composite score such as PF × win_rate / max_DD would rank trials by overall quality rather than just filtering them, surfacing the genuinely best candidates at the top. This also reduces the influence of trials that barely pass one criterion while excelling at others — a more honest picture of which parameter sets are worth assigning to a version.
Sharpe ratio should be added as a ranking/filter criterion
Current passing criteria (PF, min trades, max DD) can't distinguish between a smooth equity curve and a lumpy one with similar aggregate numbers. A 1:4 RRR strategy earns in bursts — high variability in periodic returns. A 1:1 strategy with 70% win rate is smoother. Sharpe penalises both sides equally; Sortino (downside only) may be more appropriate for high-RRR strategies. Adding Sharpe ≥ 1.0 as a filter would improve result quality.
Walk-Forward Validation
Train on a historical window, validate on out-of-sample window, slide forward. Prevents overfitting and gives statistical confidence that regime gates capture a real edge rather than noise. Highest-value next build.
Key Historical Findings
Regime Edge
The regime filter is the likely source of edge — but walk-forward will confirm or deny
The fractal entry logic and Discovery-style parameter search are not a deep moat — anyone with a backtest framework could replicate them. The regime filtering — systematically identifying which market conditions the strategy should sit out — is harder to discover by intuition and specific to this strategy. But edge is only real if regime gates hold up out-of-sample across multiple walk-forward windows. Without that, the RA is sophisticated overfitting.
Discovery
200 trials is the right minimum — 30 trials leaves too much on the table
The search space (EMA 10–200, SL 5–50, RRR 1–5, regime gate combinations) is large. 30-trial runs found 3 passing on GBPUSD; 200-trial runs found 8. The improvement is sub-linear, which is actually encouraging — it suggests passing combinations are genuinely rare rather than easy to stumble on, meaning those that do pass are more likely capturing something real.
Strategy
The strategy is genuinely asymmetric — longs do not work
Long-only Discovery runs find no passing trials. The RA macro table confirms why: staircase_up shows 9.3% win rate, strong_up shows 0.0% — these are the regimes where long fractal signals predominantly fire. This is not a code or parameter problem. Markets often fall faster than they rise, creating cleaner fractal structure on the downside. The edge appears to be short-side specific for GBPUSD/EURUSD 5m in this period.
Platform · BD / RA Role
BD and RA are now primarily out-of-sample validation and diagnostic tools
Discovery handles parameter search. BD's role is validating a Discovery candidate on a window it never saw — checking whether numbers degrade gracefully or collapse. RA's role is confirming regime gates make intuitive sense out-of-sample and diagnosing underperformance after live forward testing. Manual parameter tweaking on BD/RA is not recommended — Discovery finds better combinations than intuition.
Platform · P&L Accuracy
Sub-period re-runs produce different P&L than the parent run — this is expected
Position sizing uses equity × RISK_PCT / stop_distance. A fresh October run starts at $100k; in the 6-month run, October starts at ~$136k (compounded from earlier months). Trade counts and win rates match exactly; dollar P&L diverges. Rule: read sub-period P&L from the parent run's Monthly / Daily tables. Don't re-run to check P&L.
