Strategy Logic

Fractal Geometry Methodology

Fractal entry strategy filtered by macro and micro regimes.

1

Entry Logic

The fractal entry logic is a clean, structurally-sound signal. It's not predicting direction, it's just identifying moments where price has already demonstrated a lower-high structure, which is a meaningful read on short-term momentum. That's a solid foundation.

2

Regime Layers

The fractal regime layer is what's doing the heavy lifting in terms of selectivity. It’s essentially saying "we know this entry strategy only works when the broader market context is aligned" — and locking out the conditions where it structurally can't win.

3

Based on real market structure

What makes this particularly encouraging is that the regime classification is based on real market structure — it's not an optimised parameter, it's a description of what the market is doing. So the filter has a genuine logical basis, which makes it more likely to hold out-of-sample than if you'd just curve-fitted a time-of-day filter or something arbitrary.

FRACTAL METRICS

Each confirmed N=2 fractal records a set of real-time metrics. These are available at the moment the fractal forms — no look-ahead required — and are displayed in the Backtesting fractal table.

Swing Height — H1, H3, H6

The pip distance from the current fractal's price to the most recent opposite-kind fractal (e.g. for a high-fractal: distance to the nearest prior low-fractal). H1 is the raw value; H3 and H6 are rolling means over the last 3 and 6 non-null heights respectively, forming a smoothing ladder over the same underlying series.

H1 1 fractal Current swing size in pips — the raw leg just completed

REGIME CLASSIFICATION

The strategy uses two independent regime layers — macro and micro — both of which must be open for a trade to be taken. Both are computed in real time from live fractal data with no look-ahead.

Macro Regime Gate

The macro gate answers: is the market in an active, directional state right now? It is computed at each N=2 fractal formation using two signals that are available natively in real time.

Signal What it measures Gate threshold
H1 — current swing height Size of the most recent fractal leg in pips. Large swings indicate the market is making decisive moves. H1 ≥ T_height (default 10 pips)
ADX-14 — trend strength Directional conviction — not direction itself, but whether price is moving with purpose rather than ranging. ADX ≥ T_adx (default 20)

Both conditions must be met simultaneously. T_height and T_adx are calibrated per Discovery run — not hardcoded — so the optimal thresholds are found empirically for each instrument and date range.

Optional strict mode

When enabled, additionally requires H1 ≥ H3 ≥ H6 — expanding pre-session swing momentum. This raises precision significantly (removes ~97% of bad-day losses) at the cost of capturing fewer good days. Configurable as a toggle in Discovery settings.

Why swing height works as a macro signal

Volatility clusters — large moves tend to follow large moves, and quiet periods stay quiet. Pre-session H1 averaging 24 pips on profitable trading days vs 14 pips on unprofitable days (Cohen's d 0.58, 2025 GBPUSD sample). The combination of H1 ≥ 10 AND ADX ≥ 20 achieves 73.5% good-day capture with 68.8% bad-day exclusion — clearing the 70% viability threshold.

Note on the prior macro classifier

An earlier macro classifier based on daily N=18 fractal structure (displacement, EMA-40 slope, Williams pivots) produced strong parity-mode results but was found to have near-zero predictive power in live mode — day-to-day label persistence of only 29.1% vs a 28.4% random baseline. Its apparent performance came entirely from same-day look-ahead. The swing height + ADX classifier replaces it entirely for live trading.

Micro Regime Gate

The micro gate answers: is the immediate intraday fractal structure aligned with the trade direction? It tracks the last 4 high-fractals and last 4 low-fractals continuously, classifying the current market character in real time.

Label Description
trending_fast/medium/slow_down Both highs and lows making consistent lower-high / lower-low sequence, graded by speed
ranging_narrow/medium/wide No clear directional sequence — market oscillating, graded by range width
transitioning Regime in flux — insufficient consecutive same-kind fractals to confirm a new state
trending_fast/medium/slow_up Both highs and lows making consistent higher-high / higher-low sequence

The micro classifier uses a state machine — it does not reset at midnight. It carries fractal state continuously, which gives it natural persistence across day boundaries and makes it genuinely live-capable without modification.

Fine sub-labels (fast/medium/slow, narrow/medium/wide) are computed using four frozen threshold constants derived from training data:

Constant Value Used for
pips_per_bar_t1 0.7249 Trending slow / medium boundary
pips_per_bar_t2 1.3061 Trending medium / fast boundary
width_pips_t1 10.875 Ranging narrow / medium boundary
width_pips_t2 18.118 Ranging medium / wide boundary

When the training window is extended (e.g. regenerating regime labels for full history), these thresholds shift slightly as the quantile distribution is recomputed across a larger sample. Bake the current values into the cBot as fixed parameters; update them when a new training run produces materially different thresholds.

How the two layers interact

Both gates must be open simultaneously for a trade signal to proceed to entry. The macro gate filters by market activity and trend conviction; the micro gate filters by immediate structural alignment. Neither gate alone is sufficient.

Macro gate Micro gate Result
Open Open Signal proceeds to entry check
Open Closed Signal blocked — wrong intraday structure
Closed Open Signal blocked — insufficient market activity
Closed Closed Signal blocked

Fractal Detection

Williams N=2. A fractal at bar fi requires bars fi±1 and fi±2 to all have lower highs (fractal high) or higher lows (fractal low). Confirmation happens 2 bars later.

Entry Signals

  • Long — new fractal low forms higher than the prior fractal low
  • Short — new fractal high forms lower than the prior fractal high

Entry Reference

  • Python — close of the bar after confirmation
  • cBot — Symbol.Ask for longs, Symbol.Bid for shorts, on first tick of bar after confirmation

Stop & Target

  • Stop-loss — fractal price ± FRACTAL_STOP_PIPS offset (default 15)
  • Take-profit — entry ± (stop distance × RRR)
  • Stop validation — distance must be between MIN_STOP (5 pips) and MAX_STOP (200 pips)

Position Sizing

Formula: (equity × RISK_PCT) / stop_distance_in_price, normalised to broker volume step. RISK_PCT fixed at 1% — not a Discovery variable.

Other Rules

  • Daily loss limit — no new trades once losing-trade count reaches MAX_DAILY_LOSSES (default 2). Resets at UTC midnight.
  • Re-entry — no cooldown. A new signal on the bar after a close is accepted.
