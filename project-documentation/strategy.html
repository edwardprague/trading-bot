Regime Classification

Macro Regime

Per-day classification combining three signals: net displacement (open → close), EMA-40 slope across the day, and N=18 fractal structure.

Label Character
strong_down Sharp directional fall
staircase_down Stepped decline
flat No clear direction
staircase_up Stepped rise
strong_up Sharp directional rise

Micro Regime

Per-fractal classification. Coarse label from rolling lookback of last 4 same-kind fractals; refined into 10 fine labels via tercile thresholds (quantile-based — not absolute).

ℹ️
Fast/medium/slow and narrow/medium/wide cuts are computed as terciles across all observed periods in the run. A "fast" period in a quiet date range may not be fast by absolute standards.
v1 — Fractal Geometry Entries

Fractal Detection

Williams N=2. A fractal at bar fi requires bars fi±1 and fi±2 to all have lower highs (fractal high) or higher lows (fractal low). Confirmation happens 2 bars later.

Entry Signals

  • Long — new fractal low forms higher than the prior fractal low
  • Short — new fractal high forms lower than the prior fractal high

Entry Reference

  • Python — close of the bar after confirmation
  • cBot — Symbol.Ask for longs, Symbol.Bid for shorts, on first tick of bar after confirmation

Stop & Target

  • Stop-loss — fractal price ± FRACTAL_STOP_PIPS offset (default 15)
  • Take-profit — entry ± (stop distance × RRR)
  • Stop validation — distance must be between MIN_STOP (5 pips) and MAX_STOP (200 pips)

Position Sizing

Formula: (equity × RISK_PCT) / stop_distance_in_price, normalised to broker volume step. RISK_PCT fixed at 1% — not a Discovery variable.

Other Rules

  • Daily loss limit — no new trades once losing-trade count reaches MAX_DAILY_LOSSES (default 2). Resets at UTC midnight.
  • Re-entry — no cooldown. A new signal on the bar after a close is accepted.

v2 — Additional Gates (on top of v1)
EMA Position Filter
Toggle: USE_EMA_FILTER (default on)

Evaluated on the confirmation bar's close. Long requires close > EMA Long; short requires close < EMA Long. EMA_LONG defaults to 40 bars.

EMA Buffer
EMA40_BUFFER_PIPS — default 5 pips

Tightens the EMA filter. Short requires close < ema_long − buffer; long requires close > ema_long + buffer. Setting to 0 recovers strict-below behaviour.

Macro Regime Gate
ALLOWED_MACRO_REGIMES

Skips entries on days whose macro regime isn't in the allow-list. If the parquet doesn't cover a day, the gate passes through silently.

Micro Regime Gate
ALLOWED_MICRO_REGIMES

Skips entries whose micro regime at entry timestamp isn't in the allow-list. Labels loaded from data/regime_labels.parquet at import.
