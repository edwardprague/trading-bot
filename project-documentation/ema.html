EMA Analysis

EMAs can be valuable for gauging HTF and LTF consolidation and trending price action. At this stage we are starting with the 1h and 1m charts for multi time frame analysis, and the 200, 40, 20 EMA combination. In the image below you can see the following:

  • On the 1h chart, it's quite clear that the price is trending upwards by the 20 > 40 > 200 configuration. The strength of the trend can also be gauged by the EMA configuration.
  • The 1m chart tells quite a different, and more rapidly changing, story. In the images below the 1m chart at 10:00 looked quite different to the 1m chart at 13:30.

10:00

13:30

EMAs on the 1m chart could be useful as a measurement to gauge if the price is consolidating or trending, by considering if there has been a 20 / 40 EMA crossover in the last 10 minutes (10 candles). In the charts below, the blue boxes indicate 10m periods when the 20 crossed over the 40. The red boxes indicate 10m periods after which the price kept going. In neither case did price go far, but this illustrates a potential unique measurement idea.
