Entry Ideas

Entry Thoughts

  • Trading with the trend
  • Waiting for a Inducing PB
  • Entering when the PB gives a continuation signal

Trend Filter

  • Price > 20 > 40 > 200 (or vice versa)

Inducing PB

  • A PB with a minimum of 2 PP, against the trend direction

Continuation Signal

  • Shoulder + Head + Shoulder = Fractal 1 + Fractal 2 + Fractal 3 (enter trade)
  • Head and shoulders entry must be preceded by at least 2 PP. This would confirm that it was a long enough PB to appropriately reverse and continue from.
  • The right shoulder would be the entry signal
