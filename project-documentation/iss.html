Induction / Sweep Strategy

Methodology

The price on the 1m chart moves in macro swings which serve 2 purposes: Induction and Sweeping

This is the way that the market makes it's money on the LTF. The PP structure is very clear and consistent on the 1m chart. It's mostly simple to see and it can't foundationally be much more complex.

The sweeps are efficient, quick, and rather consistant.

Swing Types

  • Induction (Slower - More PP) - A large swing with a few handful of PBPP to an HTF PP (At the edge the remaining PP can be left for further buildup).
  • Sweep (Faster - Less PP) - PB swings should sweep a number of previous PBPP with around 3 PP legs.

Trade Types - Both After A Sweep

  • Counter Trend - At the edge of an HTF range, after a strong trend to get there.
  • Trend - An LTF micro trend with a clearish overall direction, and a multi PP leg proper sweeping PB.

Entry Questions

1

Overreaching Questions

- What does the price look like it's doing (to most people) and what is it probably doing (against most people)?

- Did price just do what most people probably thought it would not do?

- How CLEAR are the conditions? Because a trade is like a fart...

2

HTF

What is the HTF PA, and where are the PP? What does the HTF price look like it's doing (to most people) and what is it probably doing?

3

LTF

What direction makes sense on the LTF according to it's PA and the HTF PA?

4

PP Sweeps

Which PP could likely be swept sooner? Which PP could likely be swept later? (Good potential SL level)

5

Trend Entries

If trend tading, how many PBPP has the PB swing had? How many PP sweeps did it do? What is the distance to the further PP, indicating it might not continue there?

6

CT Entries

If CT trading, what HTF PP was swept? Is the price at least TP distance from the swept PP?
